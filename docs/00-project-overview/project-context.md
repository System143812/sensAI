Project:
sensAI

Purpose:
The main purpose of sensAI is to help people who are struggling mentally through lack of consistency and poor discipline on committing or having hard time to finsh or start their own self assigned tasks and plans leading to self-blame and low self esteem because of breaking the promise that they set to themselves repeatedly. SensAI helps them to focus and avoid, help to reduce their distractions and encourage them to potentially break their doomscrolling. Plus not only that but SensAI provides and AI model too that you can talk too that acts as your Sensei that teach you the way to learn and answer a thing, it won't teach you the exact answer to it without you doing any effort. This may helps us to become less dependent on AI and instead use them to boost our learning capability.

Core Problem:
AI dependency, poor time management, procrastination, and problems with self-regulation

Core Principle:
AI should help people become more capable and
independent rather than making them dependent on AI.

Target Users:
Self Learners, Learners, students, educators and anyone who's having a hard time on time-management or anyone who really wants to learn a new skill or something.
